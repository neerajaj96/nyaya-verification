## The sources

Both supplied PDFs are editions of ONE work: Annambhaṭṭa's *Tarkasaṃgraha*
with his own *Dīpikā* (Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika primer). Full bibliographic audit:
`research/source_audit.md`.

- **Edition A (PDF 1):** tr. Swami Virupakshananda, Sri Ramakrishna Math,
  Mylapore/Chennai, 2nd ed. 1994 (repr. 2001), ISBN 81-7120-674-3. Dīpikā in
  Devanāgarī + word-gloss + translation per sūtra; inference core at printed
  pp. 88–116. Best for IAST/English search.
- **Edition B (PDF 2):** ed. Y. V. Athalye, tr. M. R. Bodas, Bombay Sanskrit
  Series No. LV, Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, Poona, 2nd-ed.
  re-impression 1930. Adds Govardhana's *Nyāya-Bodhinī* + ~300 pp. of
  critical Notes (translation in italics per section); inference at Notes
  §§42–59. Best for Devanāgarī density and vyāptigraha/upādhi depth. This is
  also the edition vendored as the baseline's source-text corpus.

The PDFs are not vendored here (size + rights); the bibliography above is
sufficient to re-obtain them, and the extraction method is in `source_audit.md`.

## Doctrinal spine (inference → fallacy)

1. **Anumāna / anumiti:** inference is the *karaṇa* of inferential cognition
   (§1); anumiti is cognition born of parāmarśa **under pakṣatā** (§2 + D,
   with the saṃśayottara-pratyakṣa exclusion).
2. **Parāmarśa / pakṣadharmatā:** the fused cognition of hetu-in-pakṣa *as*
   vyāpti-qualified, stock example mountain/smoke/fire (§3, §5).
3. **Vyāpti:** *sāhacarya-niyama* ("wherever smoke, there fire"), technically
   absence-guarded same-locus co-occurrence (§4 D). Grasped via
   bhūyodarśana + vyabhicāra-absence (niścaya AND śaṅkā) + tarka/svataḥ +
   sāmānya-pratyāsatti (§7 D; vajra case refutes frequency-only induction).
4. **Two concomitances, three liṅgas:** anvaya + vyatireka with hearth/lake
   dṛṣṭāntas; kevalānvayi (jar/nameable) and kevalavyatireki (earth/odour)
   edge cases with no-dṛṣṭānta lemmas (§§11–13).
5. **Pakṣa / sapakṣa / vipakṣa:** doubt-locus / ascertained-presence-locus /
   ascertained-absence-locus (§§14–16), with the operative notion being
   pakṣatā (§2 D, §14 D).
6. **Svārtha / parārtha / pañcāvayava:** self-sequence
   (bhūyodarśana → smaraṇa → parāmarśa → anumiti, §7) vs. five-membered public
   demonstration (§§8–9) with per-member glosses and epistemic jobs
   (prayojanas) in the Dīpikā.
7. **Hetvābhāsa:** five defects with full subtypes, stock examples, and the
   two-level blocking theory — bādhita/satpratipakṣa block *anumiti*
   directly, the rest block *parāmarśa* (§§17–28, D p. 116).
8. **Upādhi:** the precise double-pervasion formula with the wet-fuel /
   red-hot-iron-ball exhibition (§27).
