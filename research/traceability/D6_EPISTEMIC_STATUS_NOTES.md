# D6 Epistemic-Status Notes — Saṃśaya / Niścaya / Siddhānta / Counterexample / Engineering-Uncertainty

These five are NOT equivalent categories. Each section stays inside what the
extracted passages support; cross-references point to the targeted files.

## 1. Saṃśaya (doubt) — sourced

- In NS 2.1.6/Bhāṣya (`targeted/ns_2_1_1_7_samsaya.md`): doubt = cognition
  arising from common-property presentation WITHOUT distinctive-feature
  apprehension; ceases on feature-perception. Five pūrvapakṣa denials
  answered; doubt is enquiry's necessary precursor (Vārttika), not
  cognition's.
- In NS 1.2.7/Bhāṣya: *cintā* (suspense) = desire-to-ascertain process from
  doubt to definitive cognition; prakaraṇasama feeds (not resolves) it.
- Status: NECESSARY-CONDITION doctrine (viśeṣa-anupalabdhi + two-sided
  alternatives), binary (doubt-or-not), protocol-embedded (2.1.7 Q&A).

## 2. Niścaya (certainty) — thinly sourced, ungraded

- Direct support: (a) ADHYAVASĀYA (conviction/certain cognition) as doubt's
  definitional opposite — "from conviction no doubt; no cause-effect
  affinity" (2.1.1 Bhāṣya); (b) 2.1.3: opposite convictions of two persons
  (soul exists/not) as the diversity-of-opinion exhibit; (c) 2.1.4 dilemma:
  uncertainty certain-or-not in its own character.
- What is NOT supported: degrees of niścaya; thresholds; probabilities;
  "high confidence" idioms; any niścaya-measurement procedure. The sources
  use niścaya/adhyavasāya as a STATUS (ascertained vs not), never a SCALE.
- TS-stratum grading (niścaya AND śaṅkā both covered by vyabhicāra-absence,
  §7 D) is LATER vocabulary with no NS ancestor evidenced — record the
  discontinuity; do not merge.

## 3. Siddhānta (established/accepted) — four-graded, dialectical

- From NS 1.1.26–28 + 2.2.1-section (`targeted/ns_2_2_1_12_siddhanta.md`):
  sarva-tantra (cross-school), prati-tantra (school-internal), adhikaraṇa
  (implication-resting), abhyupagama (assumed-for-argument).
- Siddhānta is what examination ENDS in (2.1.7 protocol + 2.2.1
  mention-then-reject completeness), NOT a certainty-degree: an abhyupagama
  can be fully certain-to-the-party yet unestablished; a sarva-tantra can be
  established yet doubted by students (Tātparya on learned-vs-student doubt).
- Consequence: ESTABLISHED varies along party/scope axes, CERTAIN varies
  along cognition axes — orthogonal dimensions, never one scale.

## 4. Counterexample (defeater) — exhibited-certainty duels

- From 2.1.37–38 (`targeted/ns_2_1_38_inference.md`): defeaters are exhibited
  (dam/nest/mimic) and are MET by finer exhibits (foam/hosts/ṣaḍja), never
  by re-weighting. The Vārttika adds a scope-match precondition (all-vs-some
  probans/subject) and an asiddha-rejection for unproved chargers.
- From 1.2.7: double non-finding neutralizes (does not refute); Unknown ≠
  Neutralised (Vārttika reply) — three distinct negative statuses:
  unproved-charger (asiddha), doubt-feeder (prakaraṇasama), certainty-defeater
  (2.1.37 exhibits).
- Upshot for D6: admission needs STATUS typing (asiddha / neutralising /
  defeating) before any force comparison; the baseline's untyped strings
  conflate all three.

## 5. Engineering uncertainty (modern, if later needed) — unsourced

- No probability, threshold, weight, or confidence-score concept exists in
  the extracted corpus. IF a future implementation needs graded handling,
  it must be labelled SOURCE_ENGINEERING_INTERPRETATION with explicit
  non-equivalence to niścaya/śaṅkā/siddhānta/saṃśaya, and D6 must be
  re-opened on those terms (it stays OPEN regardless).
