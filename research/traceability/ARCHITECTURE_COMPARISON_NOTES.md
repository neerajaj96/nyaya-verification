Task: map the new evidence's demands onto architectural families as

## Requirement classes (from Parts III–XII evidence)

- REP (representation): stratum+tradition-indexed terms; nested
  qualification/delimitation; locus-indexed absence; typed defeaters;
  evidence-lists; opponent-move typing.
- REAS (reasoning): admission-before-weighing; multi-route (not linear)
  defeat reporting; enquiry-gating distinct from assent-evaluation;
  tri-temporal causal tests.
- VER (verification): verdicts reproducible from cited strata; gold labels
  section+stratum-tagged; BLOCKED slots executable as "refuse loudly".
- PROV (provenance): readability + reconstruction-dependence + exposition/
  root split on every claim.
- INTERP (interpretation): A/B/C/D + OPEN/CONSTRAINED statuses machine-
  attached to outputs; no silent closure.
