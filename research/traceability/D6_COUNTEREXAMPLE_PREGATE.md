## 1. Provisional evidence states (sourced terms only)

Five states, each with stratum scope. States are DESCRIPTIVE labels for
sourced configurations — where a state would need new machinery it is
marked ENGINEERING_ABSTRACTION.

- S1 SAṂŚAYA-STATE [NS_EXPLICIT — 2.1.6]: common-property-without-
  distinctive-feature + live two-sided alternatives; neither side
  ascertained. Exit: feature-perception (doubt ceases).
- S2 CINTĀ-PROCESS [BHASHYA_SUPPORTED — 1.2.7]: desire-to-ascertain
  investigation running doubt → definitive cognition; fed (not resolved)
  by prakaraṇasama-type exhibits.
- S3 ADHYAVASĀYA-RECORD [BHASHYA_SUPPORTED — 2.1.1]: certain conviction of
  presence/absence on record; EXCLUDES S1 by definition (no affinity).
  Binary; ungraded.
- S4 SIDDHĀNTA-STATUS [NS_EXPLICIT — 1.1.26–28; 2.2.1-section]: four grades
  (sarva-tantra / prati-tantra / adhikaraṇa / abhyupagama). Orthogonal to
  S3: abhyupagama is certain-to-party yet unestablished.
- S5 ALLEGED-DEFEATER [VARTTIKA_SUPPORTED — 1-2-8/9 reply; 2.1.38]:
  three sourced sub-statuses, NOT one:
  (a) ASIDDHA-CHARGED — charger itself unproved (Bauddha intangible-sound;
      pūrvapakṣin's falsity-probans at 2.1.38) → REJECTED, not weighed;
  (b) NEUTRALISING — double non-finding from non-admitted loci (1.2.7
      Vārttika conditions) → feeds S2, proves nothing;
  (c) DEFEATING — exhibited certainty met by finer exhibited certainty
      (2.1.37 dam/nest/mimic met by foam/hosts/ṣaḍja) → defeats the
      unqualified claim ONLY (scope-limited: qualified claim survives).

## 2. Mandatory non-equations (future verifier MUST NOT...)

- N1: not-proven ≠ false. (S5a rejected ≠ refuted; abhyupagama unestablished
  ≠ false.) [CROSS_STRATUM_SYNTHESIS over 1-2-8/9 + 1.1.27 + 2.1.38]
- N2: not-proven ≠ counterexample. (S5b neutralises without exhibiting;
  double non-finding is not a vipakṣa exhibition.) [VARTTIKA_SUPPORTED]
- N3: retrieved contradiction ≠ classical defeater. (Only S5c exhibited,
  scope-matched defeats count; similarity scores are not exhibitions.)
  [ENGINEERING_ABSTRACTION boundary — enforced, not sourced]
- N4: absence-of-proof ≠ absence-of-fact. (Bhāṣyachandra upalabdhi/
  anupalabdhi gloss distinguishes cognising-as-possessing from
  cognising-as-not-possessing; neither is bare silence.)
  [LATER_COMMENTARY_SUPPORTED]
- N5: TS *niścita*-vipakṣa (TS §16, TARKASANGRAHA_SUPPORTED) ≠ NS exhibited
  defeater: the former adds ascertainment + same-substratum discipline the
  latter never states. Do not merge; tag per stratum.
