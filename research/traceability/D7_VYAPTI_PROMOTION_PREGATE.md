## P1 — Observed association (what is actually observed)

- NS-mode [NS_EXPLICIT]: exhibited co-occurrences at named loci — kitchen
  smoke/fire; full river/ upstream rain; ant-hosts/coming rain; ṣaḍja
  scream/peacock (1.1.5 Bhāṣya; 2.1.37–38). Observation is always
  QUALIFIED-particular, never bare correlation.
- TS-mode [TARKASANGRAHA_SUPPORTED]: repeated co-observation
  (*bhūyodarśana*, §7) — frequency language present, but ALWAYS with the
  P3 qualifiers below (vajra refutes frequency-alone).
- ENGINEERING_ABSTRACTION boundary: transaction tables / support counts are
  OUR data-structures, unattested in every stratum.

## P2 — Candidate inferential reason (more than arbitrary correlation)

- NS-mode [NS_EXPLICIT + VARTTIKA_SUPPORTED]: candidacy requires (i)
  qualification (viśeṣaṇa — 2.1.38), (ii) admitted-locus standing for any
  non-finding claim (1.2.7 Vārttika conditions), (iii) survival of
  alternative-interpretation scrutiny (1.1.5 trilemma: cause-effect texture
  accepted for smoke/fire).
- TS-mode [TARKASANGRAHA_SUPPORTED]: candidacy = vyāpya-status (hetu
  occurring where sādhya-absence is absented, §4 D guard).
- UNSUPPORTED everywhere: correlation-then-promote; resemblance-then-promote
  (baseline upamāna track is NOT a promotion path — record the firewall).

## P3 — Required checks (evidenced by current material)

- NS-mode checks (all NS_EXPLICIT or VARTTIKA_SUPPORTED):
  C1 qualification-exhibition (foam/hosts/ṣaḍja);
  C2 no-other-cause locution ("cannot be brought about by any other cause
      save rain");
  C3 never-unconcomitant locution ("never found unconcomitant with coming
      rain");
  C4 scope-match (probans must cover the WHOLE subject it is asserted of —
      Vārttika quantifier attack, else anaikāntika-adjacent failure);
  C5 charger-proof (opponent's defeater must itself be proved, else
      asiddha-rejection).
- TS-mode checks (TARKASANGRAHA_SUPPORTED): sahacāra + vyabhicāra-absence
  (niścaya AND śaṅkā) + tarka/svataḥ + sāmānya-pratyāsatti (§7 D).
- DEFERRED: trairūpya-shaped occurrence checks (G-DG-01); tarka patterns
  beyond smoke/fire (P2 Notes unsurveyed); sāmānya-mediation mechanics.

## P4 — Established status (before the project may treat the relation so)

- NS-mode: "infallible inference" exhibited (serpents/ṣaḍja) + five-factor
  displayability (1.1.32) [NS_EXPLICIT]; NO term-status, NO universal
  quantifier over unobserved cases [INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE — do not assert].
- TS-mode: vyāpti-term + guarded definition + smaraṇa-readiness (§§3–4,7)
  [TARKASANGRAHA_SUPPORTED].
- CROSS_STRATUM_SYNTHESIS (explicit): NS-established ≠ TS-established —
  different licensing conditions (qualification+exhibition vs +pakṣatā+
  blocking-clearance). A relation may be NS-established and TS-pending
  (e.g. lacking pakṣatā context) or vice versa; record BOTH flags.

## Why "frequency(H,S) high" ⇏ "vyāpti(H,S) established" (mandatory explanation)

1. [NS_EXPLICIT] 2.1.37: stocks DIE by exhibited defeater regardless of
   past frequency — frequency never stated as a licensing condition.
2. [VARTTIKA_SUPPORTED] 1.1.5 trilemma: correlation must survive
   interpretation-scrutiny (cause-effect vs inherence vs mere relation);
   frequency is interpretation-blind.
3. [TARKASANGRAHA_SUPPORTED] §7 D vajra: one hundred co-observations still
   defeated — frequency explicitly insufficient WITHOUT vyabhicāra-absence
   (niścaya + śaṅkā) + tarka/svataḥ + sāmānya.
4. [CROSS_STRATUM_SYNTHESIS] Neither stratum quantifies over unobserved
   cases from counts; NS exhibits, TS guards + discharges. A support/
   confidence number is therefore stratum-foreign in BOTH modes — its future
   use (if any) is ENGINEERING_ABSTRACTION requiring explicit
   non-equivalence labelling (cf. D6 N3).
