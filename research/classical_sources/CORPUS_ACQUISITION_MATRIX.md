## Priority rationale

- P0: NS III–V (completes the root: jāti/nigraha detail + APAVARGA-adjacent
  inference contexts) and Udayana's Pariśuddhi (asiddha origin + two-factor
  anumāna directly bear on D1/D5–D7).
- P1: Vaiśeṣika pair (ontology validation), Dignāga/Dharmakīrti (trairūpya
  comparator for any vyāpti work), Tātparyaṭīkā (Tier 1→2 bridge).
- P2: Jayanta/Bhāsarvajña (independent classical checks), Gaṅgeśa/NN
  (vyāpti machinery), apoha full-dress (one-sided now), Govardhana.
- P3: Raghuttama and below — note-level voices only.
