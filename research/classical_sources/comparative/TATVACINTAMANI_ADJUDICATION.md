### A. ROOT_TEXT/COMMENTARY passages (Sanskrit body; my parsing marked provisional where noted)

Establishes: pakṣatā + avacchedaka + āśrayāsiddhi/siddhasādhana are Gaṅgeśa's OWN working vocabulary in the opening section — Tier-5 pakṣatā isENUMERATED practice, not just TS §2 doctrine. [A for vocabulary presence; C for exact maṅgala-argument reconstruction — parsing provisional]
2. **Upādhi epistemology** (anyathākhyāti context): sādhanāvacchinna-sādhya-vyāpakatva debated; upādhitva-jñāna vs vastugatyā upādhitva DISTINGUISHED ("upādhijñāna… tad-ajñāna-daśā… vyāptigraha-sambhava; anyathā anumāna-mātra-uccheda" — else universal destruction of inference); pakṣa-side sādhyāvyāpakatā with vastugatyā upādhitva-viraha. Establishes: the suspected-vs-real upādhi distinction EXISTS at Tier 5 as doctrine — directly constrains D5 (suspected-condition cap now ROOT-ANCHORED, not merely prudent). [A]
3. **Vyāpti-jñānatva vs niścayatva** (L7070 region): anumiti-hetutva via vyāpti-JÑĀNATVA, not niścaya; saṃśayātmaka-parāmarśa LACKS vyāpti-viśiṣṭa-vaiśiṣṭya-avagāhitva (doubt-parāmarśa cannot carry vyāpti-qualified content). Establishes: doubt-state parāmarśa is inferentially sterile at Tier 5 — constrains D1 (enquiry-track consequence) and D6 (S1-state force). [A for the distinction; C for full reconstruction]
4. **Vyāpti/tail passage** (savikalpaka close): "yadā dhūmas tadā vahniḥ iti vyāptau…" with pravṛtti/niścaya/sandeha analysis — vyāpti-cognition interacting with प्रवृत्ति conditions. Recorded as LOCATED, parsing UNVERIFIED (provisional transliteration only). [D for content]
