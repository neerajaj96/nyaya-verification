## Part IX-A — HISTORICAL CLAIMS (usable as historiography, never as doctrine)

1. **School thesis**: Navya-Nyāya "founded" by Gaṅgeśa who consolidated it;
   REAL founder Udayana ("threshold to ring out the old and usher the new").
   Ancient/modern (prācīna/navya) division of Nyāyadarśana. [A as the book's
   thesis; historiographic position, debated in field — recorded, not endorsed]
2. **Structure**: 6 chapters — I Udayana/predecessors/successors (1–54);
   II pre-Gaṅgeśa (55–95: Śrīvallabha, Śivāditya, Keśava, Candra, Divākara,
   Prabhākara, Tarani, Soṇḍaḍa, Maṇikaṇṭha, Śakadhara, Nārāyaṇa, Murāri...);
   III Gaṅgeśa+Vardhamāna (96–112); IV expansion (113–170: Jīvanātha,
   Gaṅgāditya, Jayadeva-Pakṣadhara, Śaṅkara, Vācaspati II, Yajñapati...);
   V four-age (171–192); VI modern scholars (193–205: Gokulanātha,
   Giridhara, Raghunātha Thakkura, Viśvanātha Jhā, Kaviratna, Dharmadatta).
