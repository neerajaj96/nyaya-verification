## Reading notes

- The table's most load-bearing row is vyāpti/concomitance: practice at
  every acquired stratum, TERM + definition only at TS. Any formalization
  must choose its stratum or build stratum-parameterized predicates.

  four early columns, NOT_FOUND at TS — a category can be central early and
  absent late without being "renamed."
- saṃśaya inverts the pattern: root-rich, synthesis-thin — TS presupposes
  what NS theorizes, grounding D1's asymmetry warning.
