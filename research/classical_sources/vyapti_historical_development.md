# Vyāpti — Historical Development (Central Research Artifact)

The Tarkasaṃgraha formulation is NOT assumed final or universal. Each source
below speaks for itself; the trajectory is documented, not teleologized.

## 1. Gautama (Tier 0): concomitance without the term

- No *vyāpti*-term definition in acquired NS I–II. Inference works via
  perceived relations + remembrance (1.1.5), instantiated examples (kitchen/
  hearth/river/ants), and the five-factor display (1.1.32).
- Scope: three temporal/causal species; no anvaya/vyatireka vocabulary.
- Establishment: repeated observation is presupposed (kitchen), never
  theorized as *bhūyodarśana*; no tarka-discharge, no upādhi, no
 vyabhicāra-absence doctrine in the extracted window.
- Counterexample treatment: arrives as *validity attack* (2.1.37:
  dam/nest/mimic defeat stock cases), not as concomitance-failure taxonomy.
- Relata: probans/probandum functionally; hetu/sādhya terms not fixed here.

## 2. Vātsyāyana (Tier 1): remembrance made load-bearing

- Definition: none term-wise. Contribution: the *epistemics* of concomitance —
  the instance presents the concomitance-object "as if perceived" so that
  remembrance can state it (1.1.5 Bhāṣya, L3476).
- Scope: same three species; stock cases stabilized.
- Upādhi/tarka/counterexamples: not theorized as such in the extracted window.

## 3. Uddyotakara (Tier 1): concomitance under Bauddha cross-examination

- Scope/definition pressure: Bauddha trilemma — is smoke/fire concomitance
  (a) cause-effect, (b) same-substratum inherence, or (c) mere relation?
  (1.1.5 Vārttika). Nyāya side accepts (a) with effect→material-cause texture.
- Dṛṣṭānta: observed-locus (kitchen) vs inference-locus (hill) gap pressed;
  defense via remembrance + relation-perception, not via universal-quantifier
  theory.
- Hetu-relation: Dignāga-adjacent strictures on pakṣa/sapakṣa occurrence
  surface here (subsistence-in-sapakṣa-only demand) — the hetu-occurrence
  discipline later systematized as trairūpya-adjacent, but NOT so named here.
- No anvaya/vyatireka terms; no upādhi; no vyabhicāra taxonomy.

## 4. Udayana via Pariśuddhi fragments (Tier 2): the bridge

- *Vyāpyatvāsiddha* named: unknown concomitance as third asiddha (1-2-8/9
  region, L23576) — concomitance-failure becomes a *named defect class*,
  the ancestor of TS §27.
- Two-factor formula (L3487): remembrance-of-concomitance +
  recognition-of-presence — ancestor of parāmarśa's two components.
- Tarka/upādhi/full vyāptigraha: NOT evidenced in fragments — GAP (G-UD-01).

## 5. Buddhist stratum (Tier 4, recorded objections only)

- Position evidenced: Bauddha accepts *some* invariable-concomitance talk
  (presses its interpretation, not its existence, at 1.1.5 Vārttika) while
  denying inference prāmāṇya wholesale at 2.1.37 — concomitance without
  inferential authority, a combination no Nyāya stratum allows.
- Dignāga's positive account (trairūpya/pakṣadharmatā-sapakṣe-
  sattva-vipakṣe-asattva): NOT sourced — must not be reconstructed from
  Nyāya's report. Gap G-DG-01 is P1 precisely because trairūpya is the
  natural comparator for any vyāpti formalization.

## 6. Tarkasaṃgraha synthesis (Tier 6): terminologization

- Definition (§4): exoteric universal + technical guarded co-occurrence
  (*hetusamānādhikaraṇātyantābhāvāpratiyogi-sādhyasāmānādhikaraṇya*) —
  first verified occurrence of the TERM as definiendum (Phase 0).
- Scope: bivalent (anvaya + vyatireka, §§11–13) with kevala edge cases —
  categories with no NS ancestor evidenced.
- Establishment (§7 D): bhūyodarśana + vyabhicāra-absence (niścaya AND
  śaṅkā) + tarka/svataḥ + sāmānya-pratyāsatti; vajra refutes frequency-only.
- Upādhi (§27): double-pervasion formula + ayogolaka exhibition.
- Counterexamples: systematized (lake §19; vajra §7 D; ayogolaka §27).
- Hetu-relation: vyāpya-status required; failures fan out to named defects.
- Dṛṣṭānta-relation: udāharaṇa must state vyāpti + exhibit dṛṣṭānta (§9 D).

## 8. Standing verdict (unchanged, now historically situated)

Phase 0 §12 verdict stands: "search hetu(x) ∧ ¬sādhya(x)" = valid
sādhāraṇa/anvaya-violation fragment (TS §19), oversimplification stand-alone
(no vyatireka, no kevala, no niścaya/substratum, no tarka/sāmānya). The
history sharpens it: the fragment corresponds to the LATEST stratum's
anvaya-half; projecting it backward onto NS/Bhāṣya/Vārttika (which lack the
term, the bivalence, and the guard) would be anachronism, and projecting
trairūpya into it without Dignāga's text would be fabrication. Both errors
are now explicitly out of bounds.
