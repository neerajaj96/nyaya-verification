# Vyāpti — Complete Source Investigation

Question put by the brief (quoted verbatim): «Determine whether the
computational abstraction "search for x where hetu(x) ∧ ¬sādhya(x)" is
adequately supported by these sources, partially supported, or an
oversimplification.» Answer given at §12 after the evidence (§§1–11).
Nothing in this file invents doctrine; every numbered claim cites the
extraction file or a sūtra quoted there.

## 1. Definition of vyāpti

- Mūla (TS §4): `यत्र यत्र धूमस्तत्र तत्राग्निरिति साहचर्यनियमो व्याप्तिः`
  — vyāpti is *sāhacarya-niyama* (certainty of co-existence), illustrated
  "wherever smoke, there fire" (P1 PDF ~91; P2 §44; `anumana_passages.md` §3).
- Dīpikā technical definition (both editions):
  `हेतुसमानाधिकरणात्यन्ताभावाप्रतियोगिसाध्यसामानाधिकरण्यं व्याप्तिः`
  — the sādhya's co-occurrence (*sāmānādhikaraṇya*) in the same locus,
  where the sādhya is **not** the counter-entity (*pratiyogin*) of an
  absolute negation (*atyantābhāva*) co-located (*samānādhikaraṇa*) with the
  hetu. Gloss: *sāhacarya = sāmānādhikaraṇya*; *niyama* = invariability.
- Status: two registers — exoteric universal ("wherever…there…") and
  technical absence-guarded co-occurrence. Any formalization must answer to
  the second, not just the first.

## 2. Relata of vyāpti

Hetu (vyāpya — the pervaded, e.g. smoke) and sādhya (vyāpaka — the pervader,
e.g. fire), related as pervaded→pervader. The §27 upādhi case reverses the
stock direction (fire→smoke), proving the relation is **ordered**, not
symmetric (`anumana_passages.md` §9).

## 3. Relation to hetu

The hetu must be *vyāpya*: occurring in loci that (by §4's guard) lack any
absolute absence of the sādhya. A hetu that fails this is not merely weak —
it is *savyabhicāra* (§§18–21) or *viruddha* (§22) or *vyāpyatvāsiddha* (§27)
depending on the failure mode.

## 4. Relation to sādhya

The sādhya must be *vyāpaka* relative to the hetu: wherever the hetu occurs,
the sādhya co-occurs in the same substratum. The Dīpikā's guard is stated
from the sādhya side (sādhya must not be absented where hetu sits).

## 5–6. Positive concomitance (anvaya) and negative concomitance (vyatireka)

TS §§11–13 (`anumana_passages.md` §5): *hetusādhyayor āptir anvayavyāptiḥ;
tadabhāvayor āptir vyatirekavyāptiḥ* (D). Worked pair for smoke/fire:
anvaya `यत्र धूमस्तत्राग्निर्यथा महानसः`; vyatireka
`यत्र वह्निर्नास्ति तत्र धूमोऽपि नास्ति यथा महाहृदः`. Both directions are
first-class; the stock inference uses both dṛṣṭāntas (hearth + lake).

## 7–8. Anvaya and vyatireka (as inference patterns)

Three liṅga kinds: *anvayavyatireki* (both), *kevalānvayi* (anvaya only —
§12 jar/nameable, since everything is knowable there is no vyatireka),
*kevalavyatireki* (vyatireka only — §13 earth/odour, since all earth is pakṣa
there is no anvaya-dṛṣṭānta). Consequence: a vyāpti checker complete to the
source must handle reasons with **only one** concomitance and loci with
**no available dṛṣṭānta** — the "search counterexamples" pattern presupposes
the anvaya half and a populated comparison class, both of which the source
explicitly sometimes denies.

## 9. Vyāptigraha (grasping vyāpti)

TS §7 mūla: *bhūyodarśanena … mahānasādau vyāptiṃ gṛhītvā* (P1 PDF ~92).
D (P1 pp. 93–94): vyāpti is grasped by
(a) *sahacāra-jñāna* (co-observation, repeated — *bhūyodarśana*),
(b) *aided by* (*sahakṛta*) *vyabhicārajñāna-viraha* — absence of both
*niścaya* (ascertained counterexample) and *śaṅkā* (suspicion) of vyabhicāra,
(c) that absence being secured sometimes by *tarka* (for smoke/fire:
*kāryakāraṇabhāva-prasaṅga* — else the effect–cause relation breaks),
sometimes *svataḥ* (self-evidently),
(d) universal extension via *sāmānya-pratyāsatti* (generic properties
vahni-tva/dhūma-tva mediate cognition of all instances, answering the
asannikarṣa objection).
The *pārthivatva/lohalekhyatva vs. vajra* objection proves frequency alone
does not suffice (100 co-observations still defeated). See
`anumana_passages.md` §4.

## 10. Establishment of vyāpti (summary procedure in source terms)

Repeat co-observation in sapakṣa (kitchen) → active absence-of-defeater check
(no known/suspected vyabhicāra, discharged by tarka where needed) →
sāmānya-mediated universalization → vyāpti-smaraṇa at inference time
(`यत्र यत्र धूमस्तत्र तत्राग्निः इति` recalled on seeing smoke on the mountain)
→ fusion into parāmarśa (`वह्निव्याप्यधूमवानयं पर्वतः`).

## 11. Defeaters, exceptions, upādhis/qualifications, counterexamples

- Defeater genus: *vyabhicāra* (deviation) in either *niścaya* or *śaṅkā*
  form blocks vyāptigraha; *upādhi* blocks via
  *vyabhicārajñānadvārā vyāptijñānapratibandhakaḥ* (D p. 116).
- Upādhi (TS §27): *sādhyavyāpakatve sati sādhanāvyāpakatvam* — pervades the
  sādhya, does not pervade the hetu; exhibited by *ārdrendhana-saṃyoga* with
  the red-hot-iron-ball (*ayogolaka*) counterexample
  (`anumana_passages.md` §9).
- Sādhāraṇa (§19): *prameyatva* in the fireless lake defeats
  knowable→fiery. Vajra (§7 D): diamond defeats earth→scratchable despite
  100 supporting cases.
- No "exception that proves the rule" doctrine was found; defeaters destroy
  or condition the vyāpti (sopādhika ⇒ vyāpyatvāsiddha).

## 12. Verdict on "search for x where hetu(x) ∧ ¬sādhya(x)"

**PARTIALLY SUPPORTED — with three load-bearing gaps that make it, as a
stand-alone abstraction, AN OVERSIMPLIFICATION.** Point-by-point:

(a) *Supported half (anvaya-violation detection).* The Dīpikā's technical
definition is literally guarded against hetu-loci bearing the sādhya's
absolute absence, and sādhāraṇa (§19) is defined as
*sādhyābhāvavadvṛttiḥ* (occurring where sādhya is absent) with the lake as
the exhibited x. A hetu(x) ∧ ¬sādhya(x) search is therefore a legitimate
operational fragment of the sādhāraṇa check. The engine's `counterexamples[]`
+ `is_valid()` + check 7 sits on this fragment and its stock example is
correct.

(b) *Missing half (vyatireka).* The source requires the contrapositive
concomitance as an independent condition (§§11–13), with its own dṛṣṭānta
(lake for absence–absence) and with kevala cases where only one half exists.
The search pattern covers only hetu-presence/sādhya-absence and has no
representation of absence–absence checking, so it cannot distinguish
anvayavyatireki from kevalānvayi/kevalavyatireki reasons.

(c) *Missing qualifier (niścita + sāmānādhikaraṇya).*
Sapakṣa/vipakṣa require *ascertained* (*niścita*) presence/absence (§§15–16),
and vyāpti is same-substratum co-occurrence, not co-occurrence in a flat
table. A row-scan over stipulated `(locus, prop)` pairs without certainty
flags or substratum identity implements the letter (co-presence) while
dropping the qualifiers the source uses to separate knowledge from
stipulation.

(d) *Missing establishment (tarka + sāmānya + śaṅkā).*
The source's vyāptigraha is frequency **plus** defeater-absence **plus**
tarka **plus** universal-mediation (§9 above); the miner-style
support/confidence rule induction implements only the first conjunct, and the
stored-row model implements none of the last two. The vajra case is the
source's own proof that frequency-style induction without (b) fails.

Net: retain the search as the **sādhāraṇa/anvaya-violation sub-check**; do
not present it as *the* computational meaning of vyāpti. A source-faithful
vyāpti module would additionally need: (i) a vyatireka/absence–absence check
with its own dṛṣṭānta slot; (ii) kevala-tolerant logic; (iii) certainty-graded
sapakṣa/vipakṣa; (iv) an upādhi-shaped conditional check (not a bare flag);
(v) a tarka/consistency discharge step in rule learning. None of (i)–(v) is
present in either code snapshot (see `classical_to_code.md` T5/T7 and
`conflicts.md` C5–C7).

## 13. Relationship to inference (svārtha/parārtha) and to hetvābhāsa

- Inference: vyāpti-smaraṇa is the recalled major premise in svārtha (§7) and
  the udāharaṇa-stated premise in parārtha (§§8–9); parāmarśa fuses it with
  pakṣadharmatā (§3); anumiti follows (§2).
- Hetvābhāsa: vyāpti-failures fan out into four named defects — sādhāraṇa
  (broken concomitance), viruddha (pervaded-by-absence), vyāpyatvāsiddha
  (conditional concomitance), asādhāraṇa/anupasaṃhārin (doubt/no-example
  about concomitance) — with bādhita/satpratipakṣa blocking *despite* intact
  concomitance (D p. 116). Vyāpti is thus necessary but not sufficient for
  sound inference in the source's architecture.
