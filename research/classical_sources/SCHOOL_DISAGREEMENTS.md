# School Disagreement Matrix

Purpose: identify where a computational system must preserve MULTIPLE
theories, not decide correctness. Every row is evidenced in the acquired
corpus (Jha Vols. 1–2 or TS editions); unattested positions are marked TO
SOURCE, never filled from general knowledge.

## D-S1. Pramāṇa numerosity and membership

- Nyāya siddhānta (NS 2.2.1 context, Vol. 2): four — perception, inference,
  analogy, word; Lesson I proved definitional mutual-exclusivity (Parishuddhi
  via Jha p.881).
- Objector (NS 2.2.1, p.882): EIGHT — add aitihya (tradition), arthāpatti
  (presumption), sambhava (deduction/inclusion), abhāva (antithesis).
- Unattested here (to source): Buddhist-two, Cārvāka-one, Mīmāṃsā-six,
  Bhāsarvajña-reduction.
- Computational upshot: pramāṇa inventory MUST be a parameter (cf. D2); the
  baseline's fixed four + strength table silently chooses the siddhānta.

## D-S2. Inference: validity, species, structure

- Validity: Bauddha purvapaksa (2.1.37) denies inference any prāmāṇya
  (dam/nest/mimic); Nyāya siddhanta defends (2.1.38, unextracted).
- Species: NS trividha (pūrvavat/śeṣavat/sāmānyatodṛṣṭa) vs TS
  svartha/parartha + liṅga-traividhya — different organizations, both
  sourced; no harmonization attempted.
- Structure: NS five factors (1.1.32) vs TS five members + prayojanas (§9);
  Vārttika notes factor-interdependence (nigamana presupposes ascertained
  concomitance).
- Computational upshot: proof-object schema must be stratum-tagged (NS-mode
  vs TS-mode); the baseline emits TS-mode only.

## D-S3. Vyāpti / concomitance (see vyapti_historical_development.md)

- Nyāya strata: presupposed (NS) → remembrance-theoretic (Bhāṣya) →
  defended under trilemma (Vārttika: cause-effect texture) → terminologized
  + guarded + bivalent + upādhi-qualified (TS).
- Bauddha (reported): concomitance-talk accepted for scrutiny while
  inference-authority denied; sapakṣa-only occurrence demanded.
- Trairūpya: NOT SOURCED — the Buddhist positive account is Gap G-DG-01.
- Computational upshot: no single vyāpti predicate; D5–D7 must resolve per
  stratum or explicitly scope to TS-mode.

## D-S4. Apoha / universals (word-meaning; perception-adjacent)

- Nyāya (NS 2.2.59+, Vol. 2 pp.1036–1066): word denotes
  vyakti/ākṛti/jāti (individual/configuration/generality); four inferences
  prove cow-generality distinct from individuals (comprehensive-cognition,
  subject-of-conception, subject-of-name, distinct-cause arguments).
- Bauddha (reported objections): denotation = apoha (negation of contrary);
  dilemmas on 'sat' (generality? object? relation? qualified individual?).
- Computational upshot: positive-denotation vs exclusion semantics for any
  future lexical/classification component (cf. baseline upamāna's
  resemblance semantics — TS-side only, apoha-side unimplemented and
  unsourceable from Nyāya's report alone).

## D-S5. Perception definition

- NS (1.1.4): contact-produced, inexpressible, non-erratic, determinate.
- Dignāga (reported, refuted Vol. 1 pp.150–152): kalpanāpoḍha (free from
  determination/construction).
- Jaimini (reported, refuted): contact-only (fails: contact also yields
  doubt/error). Sense-function definition (reported, refuted): enumerates
  organs (fails partial-organ cases).
- Computational upshot: perception-fact admission criteria differ by school;
  baseline's "pratyakṣa = strongest tag" is school-unmarked (D2).

## D-S6. Negation (abhāva): pramāṇa vs padārtha

- Objector (2.2.1): abhāva as 8th pramāṇa (antithesis).
- Nyāya/TS: abhāva as padārtha (TS fourfold; NS 16-list member), with
  atyantābhāva-idiom inside TS vyāpti-guard.
- Siddhānta reply: NOT EXTRACTED — do not assert how Nyāya reconciles
  abhāva's dual roles.
- Computational upshot: the vyāpti-guard's absence-idiom and a
  pramāṇa-theoretic abhāva are DIFFERENT abhāva-roles; merging them
  (e.g. treating guard-absences as perceptual reports) is unsourced.

## D-S7. Inference structure display (nigamana/pratijñā function)

- Vārttika-region debate: final conclusion assertable only after
  concomitance ascertained beyond dispute; proposition states as to-be-
  proved vs conclusion asserts as proved; TS adds abādhitatva prayojana.
- Computational upshot: proof-object lifecycle (stated → ascertained →
  asserted) needs stage-marking; baseline generates all five members
  simultaneously with no stage discipline.

## D-S8. Hetvābhāsa boundaries

- Old fivefold (NS 1.2.4) vs new fivefold (TS §17) — different members
  (see hetvabhasa_historical_development.md non-collapse ledger).
- Live boundary dispute in-corpus: Bauddha intangible-sound exhibit →
  Vārttika says indecisive, not unknown-probans (with three diagnostics).
- Kālātīta drift noted by Jha (p.561); jāti redefined sans-concomitance by
  "modern Logicians" (Vol. 1 L24467).
- Computational upshot: fallacy labels are stratum-relative; cross-stratum
  evaluation sets must not mix gold labels.

## D-S9. Certainty (niścaya vs śaṅkā)

- TS §7 D: vyabhicāra-absence covers BOTH niścaya and śaṅkā, discharged by
  tarka or svataḥ.
- NS 2.1.37: stock defeats are exhibited certainties (dam/nest/mimic),
  not suspicion-grades.
- Graded-certainty machinery: NOT SOURCED anywhere acquired.
- Computational upshot: D6's niścaya question cannot be answered "more
  Bayesianism" from these texts; the sourced distinction is
  niścaya/śaṅkā/absence, discharged by tarka — a logical, not probabilistic,
  operation (pending Udayana acquisition).

## D-S10. Causation (cause-effect texture of concomitance)

- Vārttika (1.1.5): smoke/fire concomitance accepted AS cause-effect
  textured (effect→material-cause), against Bauddha mere-relation option.
- TS §27: upādhi analysis presupposes discriminating genuine from
  conditional concomitance (fire→smoke needs wet-fuel).
- Computational upshot: concomitance ≠ correlation; any miner output needs
  a texture test (tarka-shaped) before promotion — reinforces D7.

## D-S11. Linguistic testimony (śabda/āpta)

- NS 1.1.7: āptopadeśa (preceptor utterance); 2.2: word/sound sections,
  non-eternality vs Mīmāṃsā (detail unextracted).
- TS §59: āpta = yathārthavaktā (Phase 0).
- 1930-edition-as-āpta (baseline): UNSOURCED equation (D9).
- Computational upshot: testimony-admission needs speaker-credibility +
  content checks per stratum; similarity scores are neither.

## Multi-theory preservation requirements (for any future system)

1. Stratum tags on every verdict (NS/Bhāṣya/Vārttika/TS + objector voices).
2. Parameterized pramāṇa inventory (D-S1) and fallacy taxonomy (D-S8).
3. Dual proof-object modes (D-S2, D-S7).
4. Exclusion-semantics track kept open but unimplemented until Buddhist
   texts are acquired (D-S4).
5. Certainty as niścaya/śaṅkā/tarka-discharge, not probabilities (D-S9).
