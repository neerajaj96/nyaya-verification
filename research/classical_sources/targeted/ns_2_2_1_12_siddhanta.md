## BHĀṢYA on 2.2.1 [P. 100–104]

- Four objector-definitions: (A) AITIHYA — "so they say" handed down WITHOUT
  definitely-known asserter; (B) ARTHĀPATTI — implication: asserted fact ⇒
  implied fact (stock: "no rain when no clouds" ⇒ "rain when clouds");
  (C) SAMBHAVA — cognition following cognition "invariably concomitant"
  (weights: quarter-maund → two-and-half-seers → seer); (D) ABHĀVA —
  contrast existent/non-existent (non-raining ⇒ cloud–high-wind connection
  obstructing gravity-driven drops; Bhāṣyachandra: cause-potency = presence).
- Attribution dispute: Bhāṣya — objector ADMITS eight (Paurāṇikas, "who alone
  postulate eight"); Vārttika — may be an HONEST ENQUIRER doubting the number
  after hearing others posit them; Bhāṣyachandra — sūtra puts forward the
  eight-pramāṇa VIEW.
- NOTE: sambhava defined via "invariably concomitant" — concomitance
  language inside the pramāṇa-count debate.

## ROOT_TEXT — NS 1.1.26–28 (siddhānta classification; Vol. I, Jha p.345–346)

- Sū. 26: doctrine (siddhānta) = conviction regarding the real nature of a
  thing (*abhyupagama*-adjacent definition; Bhāṣya: "this is so, this thing
  has such character" — general + detailed idea).
- Sū. 27: FOUR KINDS — (1) common to all philosophies (sarva-tantra),
  (2) peculiar to one philosophy (prati-tantra), (3) resting on implication
  (adhikaraṇa), (4) hypothetical/assumed (abhyupagama).
- dispute (Vārttika/Tātparya via Jha): is 26 definition or division?
  (definition-words vs division-words argument; Tātparya reconciles Bhāṣya
  with Vārttika; Jha adopts the reconciliation — TRANSLATOR_NOTE judgment,
  flagged as such).

## Analysis — established vs proposed vs uncertain (no modern terms imposed)

- The corpus supports a FOUR-WAY sourced distinction: (i) sarva-tantra —
  cross-school established; (ii) prati-tantra — school-internal established;
  (iii) adhikaraṇa — implication-resting (established-conditional);
  (iv) abhyupagama — assumed-for-argument (hypothetical, NOT established).
- Established doctrine relates to doubt thus: doubt TRIGGERS examination
  (2.1.7), and examination can END in sarva/prati-tantra establishment; the
  2.2.1 section exists to convert enquirer-doubt into rejection-with-reasons
  (mention-then-reject completeness).
- CANDIDATE vs ESTABLISHED vs UNCERTAIN: SUPPORTED as a source distinction
  with THESE senses — candidate ≈ pūrvapakṣa-view or eight-view under test;
  established ≈ siddhānta (four senses above); uncertain ≈ saṃśaya-state
  (2.1.1–7) or prominence of *anupalabdhi*-style non-cognition (Bhāṣyachandra
  upalabdhi/anupalabdhi gloss). It is NOT supported as: probabilities,
  thresholds, or a single linear scale. Abhyupagama (assumed) is the closest
  sourced analogue of a provisional engineering status — but it is a
  DIALECTICAL role (assumed by a party), not a confidence value.
- Siddhānta-vs-pramāṇa: Lesson-I exclusivity proves definitions, not number
  (Parishuddhi) — a sourced warning against inferring inventory from
  definitions, directly relevant to baseline's fixed-four inventory.
