## Passage 1 — Negative-concomitance debate, 3.1.1–3 (Jha pp. ~1102–1103)

- SOURCE_VĀRTTIKA (+Tātparya/Pariśuddhi placement dispute IN-TEXT):
  objector demands positive concomitance (absence-of-restriction with
  Intelligence) for the soul-proof; reply: the probans is NEGATIVE —
  its force is "the infallibility or invariable character of its NEGATIVE
  concomitance," i.e. "universality of the negative relation," evidenced by
  "everything non-intelligent is restricted as to object."
- Counter-challenge + reply: if purely-negative probans sufficed, why not
  "odorous → earth eternal" (Water/Air etc. non-odorous are non-eternal =
  true universal negative concomitance)? Reply: odorousness is "precluded
  from both" (neither invariably concomitant with eternal NOR non-eternal) —
  an asādhāraṇa-shaped exclusion WITHOUT the term.
- Tātparya restatement (IN-TEXT): intelligent = permeating all cognitions
  (right/doubtful/wrong/reminiscential); independent + unrestricted.
- Bhāṣyachandra glosses ("I who saw colour now smell odour"; distinctive
  feature reading). Pariśuddhi: section-bridge to 3.1.4 (body-as-agent
  objection preview).
- Relevance: FIRST Tier-0/1 evidence of (a) vyatireka-PRACTICE with
  universality language, (b) purely-negative-probans defense, (c) double-
  exclusion reasoning. Terms anvaya/vyatireka/vyāpti still absent. Directly
  bears on D7 (vyatireka half no longer TS-only as practice) and D5
  (conditional-concomitance reasoning without formula). Does NOT revise the
  TS-only status of the TERMS (CROSS_STRATUM_EVIDENCE stands, practice
  cells upgraded — see impact file).
