# Anumāna — Historical Development Across the Acquired Corpus

No synthetic "single Indian theory" is constructed here. Each stratum keeps
its own organization; transitions record retention/change/introduction/
dispute ONLY where the acquired corpus evidences them. Unacquired strata
(Tier 2 independents, Buddhist root texts, Navya-Nyāya) appear as gaps, not
as reconstructed positions.

## Gautama (NS, Tier 0 — acquired I–II)

- Definition by genus + three species (SOURCE_ROOT_TEXT, NS 1.1.5, Jha Vol. 1
  p.153): *athatatpūrvakaṃ trividham anumānaṃ pūrvavac cheṣavat
  sāmānyatodṛṣṭaṃ ca*. Inference is led up to by perception; its internal
  division is temporal/causal (before/after/uniformity-observed), NOT
  svartha/parartha.
- Public form fixed separately (SOURCE_ROOT_TEXT, NS 1.1.32, Vol. 1 p.955):
  five factors — proposition, probans, example, re-statement (application),
  final conclusion. No svartha/parartha distinction at the root; no
  parāmarśa/pakṣatā vocabulary.
- Validity contested in-stratum (SOURCE_ROOT_TEXT, NS 2.1.37–38, Vol. 2
  pp.798–799): purvapaksa *anumānam apramāṇam* (obstruction/demolition/
  resemblance defeat the three stock cases); siddhanta replies (2.1.38 text
  not extracted — transition to reply unsourced).
- Terminology retained forward: probans/probandum (hetu/sādhya functional
  ancestors), dṛṣṭānta (16 padārthas, NS 1.1.1), traividya
  pūrvavat/śeṣavat/sāmānyatodṛṣṭa.

## Vātsyāyana (Bhāṣya, Tier 1 — acquired in full I–II)

- Retained: NS definitions, trividha scheme, five factors, stock examples.
- Introduced/sharpened: *tatpūrvaka* unpacked as perception-of-relation +
  remembrance-of-probans (1.1.5 Bhāṣya); the example/instance given an
  epistemic job — presenting the concomitance-object "as if perceived" so
  remembrance can work (L3476). Debate examples stabilized
  (clouds/ants/river/peacock at 1.1.5 and 2.1.37).
- Changed: nothing doctrinally reversed in the extracted window; Bhāṣya
  extends by exemplification and by answering which perception grounds which
  inference.

## Uddyotakara (Vārttika, Tier 1 — acquired in full I–II)

- Retained: Bhāṣya's two-element cognition (relation-perception +
  remembrance).
- Sharpened under attack: Bauddha smoke/fire trilemma (cause-effect vs.
  same-substratum-inherence vs. mere relation) at 1.1.5 Vārttika — concomitance
  must survive alternative-interpretation scrutiny; first alternative
  (cause-effect texture) accepted for smoke/fire.
- Changed (exegetical): peacock-scream case re-read as cloud-inference so the
  three stock cases span past/present/future symmetrically (2.1.37 Vārttika
  note). Final-conclusion interdependence debated (five factors mutually
  presupposing; nigamana-entry L18747).
- Introduced: systematic Bauddha-voice engagement (Dignāga-adjacent
  pakṣa/sapakṣa strictures surface here and in hetvābhāsa sections).

## Vācaspati / Udayana (Tier 2 — fragments via Jha notes only)

- Vācaspati (Tātparyaṭīkā fragments): *apramāṇam* gloss at 2.1.37 (even wrong
  premises yield *some* cognition; *a-* = never definite-right-cognition);
  vyakti/ākṛti/jāti note in apoha debate. No full position reconstructible —
  GAP.
- Udayana (Pariśuddhi fragments — load-bearing):
  (a) two-factor formula "remembrance of invariable concomitance +
  recognition of probans-presence" (L3487) — the direct ancestor of TS
  parāmarśa's two components, still without the term;
  (b) threefold asiddha (unknown subject/probans/concomitance — L23576),
  the taxonomy TS §§24–27 adopts wholesale. This is the single verified
  Tier 1→TS doctrinal bridge for inference machinery.
- Disputed/unclear: how much of TS's pakṣatā apparatus (if any) has
  Pariśuddhi precedent — NOT evidenced; flagged for G-UD-01 acquisition.

## Buddhist stratum (Tier 4 — objections recorded, texts not acquired)

- Bauddha voice inside Nyāya texts: denies inference prāmāṇya (2.1.37);
  presses concomitance alternatives (1.1.5 Vārttika); reclassifies Nyāya's
  fallacy exhibits (intangible-sound as indecisive-not-unknown).
- Dignāga: kalpanāpoḍha perception definition refuted (1.1.4 discussion);
  sapakṣa-only subsistence strictures cited. Trairūpya as such NOT sourced —
  do not reconstruct it from these fragments.

- "Modern Logicians" redefine jāti sans-concomitance (Vol. 1 L24467): evidence

## Tarkasaṃgraha synthesis (Tier 6 — acquired, Phase 0)

- Retained: mountain/smoke/fire stock matter; kitchen/hearth observed locus;
  five-membered public form (NS 1.1.32 → TS §9 with added per-member glosses
  + prayojanas); asiddha threefold (via Udayana fragment).
- Changed: trividha pūrvavat/śeṣavat/sāmānyatodṛṣṭa organization DROPPED in
  favor of svartha/parartha + liṅga-traividhya (anvaya-vyatireki/
  kevalānvayi/kevalavyatireki, §§11–13); hetvābhāsa list REPLACED (old
  prakaraṇasama/sādhyasama/kālātīta out; satpratipakṣa/bādhita/asiddha-group
  in); vyāpti TERMINOLOGIZED with guarded definition (§4 D).
- Introduced: anumiti, parāmarśa, pakṣatā, vyāptigraha-as-tarka doctrine,
  upādhi formula (§27), two-level blocking theory (p.116) — none verified in
  NS/Bhāṣya/Vārttika acquired text.
- Interpreted differently: dṛṣṭānta (from padārtha/debate slot in NS to
  vyāpti-instantiating example in TS §9 D); pakṣa (no NS definition → TS
  doubt + pakṣatā-substratum double account §§14/§2 D).
