# Hetvābhāsa — Historical Development (Chronological Comparison)

Categories are NOT collapsed across periods. Modern-logic resemblances name
nothing here. Each taxonomy keeps its own members, definitions, conditions,
examples, exclusions, and commentary.

## Taxonomy A — Gautama, NS 1.2.4–9 (Tier 0, acquired; Jha English verified,
Sanskrit compounds OCR-degraded)

- Members (standard enumeration; Jha renderings verified in place):
  (1) savyabhicāra — "Indecisive/Erratic Probans" (Maitra's-child/darkness:
  accidental relation, explicable otherwise);
  (2) viruddha — contrary reason;
  (3) prakaraṇasama — equal-context (proves both sides' contexts);
  (4) sādhyasama — unproved/sādhya-like reason;
  (5) kālātīta — "Belated/Mistimed" (1.2.9 verified p.560–561:
  sound-eternal-because-manifested-by-conjunction; manifestation
  pre/post-exists, so the probans is "behind time").
- Conditions: per-sūtra (1.2.5–9); detail partially extracted (kalātīta +
  asiddha-region read; prakaraṇasama/sādhyasama wording not recovered).
- Examples: Maitra's dark child; sound/visibility; sound-intangible
  (Bauddha exhibit, reclassified); sound-manifested-by-conjunction/colour.
- Exclusions/boundary disputes (in-corpus): Bauddha "sound-eternal-because-
  intangible" offered as unknown-probans counterexample → Vārttika
  reclassifies as INDECISIVE, adding three diagnostics (meaning of sūtra,
  literal significance, probans-vs-example defect confusion). Category
  boundaries were thus contested at Tier 1, not given.
- Commentary: Vārttika adjudications per sūtra; Jha note that the kalātīta
  conception "undergone change" (p.561) — drift documented, direction
  pending Vol. 3/Tier 2 texts.
- Differences vs later: NO satpratipakṣa/bādhita members; NO asiddha
  threefold; prakaraṇasama/sādhyasama/kālātīta have NO TS counterpart names
  (functional overlaps are hypotheses, not identities — see ledger).

## Taxonomy B — Udayana fragment, threefold asiddha (Tier 2, via Jha notes)

- Members: āśrayāsiddha (subject unknown) / svarūpāsiddha (probans itself
  unknown) / vyāpyatvāsiddha (concomitance unknown) — Pariśuddhi gloss at
  NS 1-2-8/9 (L23576–23579).
- Status: transmitted as gloss, not root; the ONLY verified Tier 0→TS
  taxonomy bridge. Whether Udayana innovated or transmitted it is NOT
  evidenced (G-UD-01).

## Taxonomy C — Annambhaṭṭa, TS §§17–28 (Tier 6, Phase 0)

- Members: savyabhicāra (sādhāraṇa/asādhāraṇa/anupasaṃhārin) / viruddha /
  satpratipakṣa / asiddha (āśraya/svarūpa/vyāpyatva) / bādhita — with stock
  examples (lake, śabdatva, omni-pakṣa, kṛtakatva, śrāvaṇatva/kāryatva,
  sky-lotus, visibility, wet-fuel/iron-ball, fire-cold) and the p.116
  two-level blocking theory.
- Differences vs Taxonomy A: satpratipakṣa + bādhita + grouped-asiddha are
  NEW members; prakaraṇasama/sādhyasama/kālātīta are GONE as names;
  viruddha/savyabhicāra names persist with reworked contents.

## Consequence for any HetvabhasaClassifier (future, NOT implemented)

The classifier must be stratum-parameterized (A-mode vs C-mode minimum, B as
annotated bridge), must refuse prakaraṇasama/sādhyasama/kālātīta inputs in
C-mode and satpratipakṣa/bādhita inputs in A-mode, and must log the active
taxonomy on every verdict. A single five-label head is historically false.
