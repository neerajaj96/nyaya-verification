## Required text

Dignāga's *Pramāṇasamuccaya* Chapter II (svārthānumāna — inference for
oneself), specifically the trairūpya passage (v. II.5cd + auto-commentary
PSV ad loc., per the scholarly consensus pointer below), PLUS the hetu-
lakṣaṇa context (pakṣadharmatā / sapakṣe sattva / vipakṣe asattva) needed to
compare occurrence-disciplines with Nyāya sapakṣa/vipakṣa and TS traividya.

  discipline (sapakṣa-presence/vipakṣa-absence) as "the" promotion rule
